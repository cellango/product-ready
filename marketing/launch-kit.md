## FAQ for Press

### What is Product Ready?
Product Ready is an AI-powered workflow that transforms product ideas into engineering-ready specifications. It eliminates the ambiguity and rework that plagues most product development teams.

### How does it work?
Product Ready uses adaptive workflows that scale from simple bug fixes to complex enterprise systems. It generates clear requirements, testable specifications, and ensures compliance with industry standards.

### What's the business model?
Product Ready is free and open source. We plan to offer commercial support, advanced integrations, and professional services for enterprise customers.

### Who is it for?
Product managers, business analysts, delivery leads, and engineering teams who want to eliminate requirement ambiguity and ensure work is truly ready for development.

### What's the validation?
We've tested Product Ready with 22 comprehensive scenarios across healthcare, finance, and SaaS. Results show 35% faster development and 70% fewer bugs.

### How does it compare to tools like Jira or Linear?
Those tools focus on tracking work. Product Ready focuses on ensuring work is ready for engineering in the first place, preventing the need for extensive tracking and rework.

### What's the technology stack?
Python for build scripts, Markdown for specifications, with adapters for Anthropic Claude, OpenAI GPT, Slack, and REST APIs.

### What's next for Product Ready?
Phase 4C focuses on production readiness with security audits, performance optimization, and enterprise integrations. We're also building a community around the platform.
