# Example: Prioritization - CSV Export vs Other Features

## Feature Evaluation

### CSV Export
- **Impact:** High (80% admin satisfaction increase)
- **Effort:** Low (2-3 days dev)
- **Risk:** Low (simple feature)
- **Confidence:** High (clear requirements)

### Alternative: Email Reports
- **Impact:** Medium (50% satisfaction)
- **Effort:** Medium (1 week dev)
- **Risk:** Medium (email deliverability)
- **Confidence:** Medium (needs validation)

### Alternative: Advanced Filtering
- **Impact:** High (90% satisfaction)
- **Effort:** High (2 weeks dev)
- **Risk:** High (complex UI)
- **Confidence:** Low (unclear scope)

## Ranked Backlog
1. **CSV Export** - High impact, low effort, quick win
2. **Advanced Filtering** - High impact, but high risk/effort
3. **Email Reports** - Medium impact, medium effort

## Decision Rules
- Prioritize if Impact/Effort ratio > 2
- Defer if Risk > Medium and Confidence < High
